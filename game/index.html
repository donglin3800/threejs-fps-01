EMBER YARD

A round-based tactical shooter in a dusk-lit industrial yard.

You are Ember — the attacking team. Fight through lanes A, mid or B with three teammates, plant the bomb at Site A, and hold it until it blows. Slate defends: four bots hold the lanes, hunt noise, and rush to defuse. First to 5 rounds takes the yard. Earn cash for kills and round wins, and spend it between rounds on rifles, a marksman rifle, grenades and armour. Standing still and aiming beats spraying on the move.
